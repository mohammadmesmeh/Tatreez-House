Every stitch carries a story.

Tatreez House imagines a small Palestinian fashion house where tatreez is worn every day, not kept in a drawer. Our pieces bring traditional cross-stitch motifs onto contemporary dresses, accessories and home textiles.

Wear it. Learn it.

We make things for two kinds of people: those who want to wear tatreez, and those who want to stitch it themselves. Alongside the collection, we share downloadable patterns, a growing journal and workshops for every level.

Where we're going

Our long-term goal is to work with Palestinian makers and to offer workshops that go beyond the basics, so that people can tell their own story through stitching.

Tatreez House is a fictional brand created for a portfolio project. Products, prices and plans on this site are demonstration content.
