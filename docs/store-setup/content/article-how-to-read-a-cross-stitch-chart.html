Every tatreez pattern starts as a chart: a grid where each square is one stitch. Once you can read it, any motif is within reach.

One square, one stitch

Each square on the chart equals one cross-stitch on your fabric. Count the squares, count the threads, and the design will appear exactly as drawn.

Colours and symbols

Every colour has its own symbol, so you can follow the chart even when two shades look alike. The thread list tells you which floss matches each symbol.

Find the centre

Small arrows on the edges of the chart mark its centre. Fold your fabric in half both ways to find its middle, and start stitching there, so the design sits evenly on the cloth.

Count in tens

Bold lines divide the chart into blocks of ten stitches. Work one block at a time, and check your count at each bold line.

Keep your crosses neat

Make every top stitch slant in the same direction. It's a small habit, but it gives tatreez its smooth, even look.
