Thank you for shopping with Tatreez House. This policy explains how we ship your order, what it costs, and what to expect after you buy.

Order Processing
Orders are processed within 2–4 business days. You'll receive a confirmation email as soon as your order is placed, and a second email with tracking when it ships, if the shipping method supports it.

Digital Patterns
Digital patterns are delivered instantly. After payment you'll see a download link on the order confirmation page, and we'll email the same link to you. Nothing is shipped for digital items, and no shipping fee is charged for them.

Shipping Methods & Delivery Times
Available shipping methods and estimated delivery times are shown at checkout. Delivery times are estimates, not guarantees, and begin once your order ships. They may be affected by carrier delays, weather, or other events outside our control.

Shipping Costs
Standard shipping is $5 within Palestine and $15 internationally. International orders over $150 ship free. The final shipping cost is always shown at checkout before you pay.

Order Tracking
Once your order ships, we'll email you a tracking number and link, if applicable. Tracking information may take some time to update.

Shipping Destinations
We ship within Palestine and internationally. International orders may be subject to customs duties, import taxes and fees, which are the customer's responsibility unless they are collected at checkout. Any charges collected at checkout are shown before you complete your purchase.

Address Accuracy
Please check your shipping address at checkout. Contact us as soon as possible if you need to correct it.

Lost, Damaged, or Delayed Packages
If your order is lost, damaged, or delayed, contact us and we will help you resolve it with the carrier.

Order Changes & Cancellations
Need to change or cancel your order? Contact us as soon as possible. We can usually make changes before your order ships, but once it's in transit we're unable to modify it. Refunds for cancelled orders are processed to the original payment method.

Questions?
Contact us using the details on our Contact page.
